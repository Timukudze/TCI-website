About TCI

Our Story

Decade of Great Works & Exploits

Tabernacle of Christ International (TCI) was founded on 29th January 2006 in Zimbabwe. What began as a single assembly has grown into a global movement spanning 18 countries across Africa and Asia.

2026 marks the 10th Year of our Decade of Great Works & Exploits (2017–2027). We are in the Second Storm of our Provincial Onslaughts, executing the 2x2 Strategy to fulfill the Great Commission.
